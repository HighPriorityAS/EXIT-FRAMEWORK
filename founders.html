What Founder means

Belonging, not access.

The core stays open. Founder value comes from identity, belonging, provenance and participation — and from the fact that one hundred never becomes one hundred and one.

01 / Identity

A number that stays yours.

FOUNDER / 0XX OF 100 marks a permanent position in EXIT's founding cohort. It is not a subscription tier.

02 / Belonging

A cohort with a beginning and an end.

One hundred people share the same founding moment, the same language and a closer line into the build. When the cohort closes, it stays closed.

03 / Provenance

A verifiable place in the record.

Each position receives a serialized Founder number and permanent founder record. Public attribution remains optional.

04 / Scarcity

The system may scale. The founding cohort does not.

No Founder 250. No Founder 1000. No second founding series. Scarcity is structural, not manufactured urgency.

Founder rules / non-negotiable

Scarcity is a promise we make before success.

If EXIT grows, the founding cohort should become more meaningful — never larger. The rules exist now so success cannot dilute them later.

Hard capExactly 100 Founder positions.
