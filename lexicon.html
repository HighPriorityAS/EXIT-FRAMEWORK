EXIT Language / v1.0 / Public

Name the pattern. Change the system.

EXIT Language is a public vocabulary for human autonomy under load. Its job is to make load, capacity, constraints, choice and recovery legible enough to discuss, design around and act on.

System > motivationState before storyShrink the control surfaceRe-entry beats restart

Core equation

CHAOS → CONVICTION → CONTROL

Conviction is enough clarity to choose without pretending uncertainty is gone.

Chaos

Competing unresolved load.

Too many signals, demands, loops or constraints competing for limited capacity.

Conviction

Enough clarity to choose.

A direction becomes actionable without requiring certainty.

Control

The ability to choose.

Usable agency under constraint: choose, execute, observe and adjust.

Canonical terms

A shared vocabulary for recurring system states.

Active constraint

The bottleneck that matters now.

The condition most limiting safe, useful progress in the current interval.

Control surface

What you are trying to manage.

The visible variables, decisions, obligations and open loops competing for attention.

Control point

The lowest-force leverage point.

The earliest place where a small intervention can change a larger loop.

Minimum Viable Control

The smallest system that preserves agency.

The minimum conditions and actions needed to keep meaningful choice available today.

Graceful degradation

Reduce scope, preserve function.

As capacity falls, the system becomes smaller without losing its essential purpose.

Drift

Direction changes without a decision.

Gradual movement away from the chosen protocol without deliberate choice.

Re-entry

Continue from reality now.

No restart ritual. No replaying missed steps. No artificial catch-up debt.

Protected base

Do not finance output with collapse.

Safety, recovery and other minimum conditions that should not be sacrificed for short-term output.

Not Now

Valid does not mean active.

An explicit holding area for legitimate demands outside the current control surface.

Proof Loop

Action → evidence → review → adjustment.

Confidence becomes more trustworthy when it is built from observable action rather than intensity.

Low-capacity mode

Narrow the load deliberately.

Reduce visible demands, limit choices, protect the base and preserve one useful move.

Recovery

Restore operating capacity.

Recovery belongs inside the operating model because it restores the capacity required for choice.

Boundary

Operational language is not scientific validation.

EXIT Language is a product and communication vocabulary. Claims from research must be evaluated separately.

Research participation is not sold.

Human Autonomy Study remains a separate research initiative.
