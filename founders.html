Founder thesis

Founders get permanent identity, early access and documented influence.

The offer should remain simple enough to understand in one minute. Founder 100 is a one-time access product that installs the core OS and includes the first year of the Control Room. Early prototypes and physical drops are secondary benefits — not reasons to buy a system you do not need.
