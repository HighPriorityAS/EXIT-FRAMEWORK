FAQ

Clear answers before checkout.

The launch is intentionally explicit about what is included now, what is planned, and what remains separate.

What is Exit Framework?

A personal operating system for identifying the active sources of chaos, stabilizing the base, choosing the highest-leverage next move, executing it, measuring the result and improving the system over time.

What is Founder 100?

The first 100 paid commercial members. Founder 100 includes Exit OS, the launch implementation stack, 12 months of Control Room, a permanent founder number, build-log access and priority for future Field Layer beta invitations.

Is 4 900 NOK the live price?

It is the current configuration value. The site can change it centrally before payment activation. No payment occurs until a verified Stripe Payment Link is configured.

Are the hoodie and field bag already in production?

No. Founder Drop 01 is reservation-first. Paid inventory should open only after samples, unit economics, sizing, fulfilment and returns are validated.

Are Oura or AR discounts included?

Not yet. They are planned partner benefits only. They must not be shown as confirmed until written partner or affiliate terms exist.

What is Exit Field Layer?

A future ambient interface layer for bringing missions, status and context-aware prompts into wearable or AR environments. It is not required for the current Exit Framework launch.

Does Founder 100 include a place in Human Autonomy Study?

No. Research participation cannot be purchased, bundled or guaranteed. Founder members may opt into early recruitment notifications, while actual participation remains subject to study protocol, eligibility, consent and capacity.

Is Exit Framework therapy or medical treatment?

No. Exit Framework is a self-management and operating-system product. It should not be presented as diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for medical or mental-health care.
