Human Autonomy Study

Research stays separate from commerce.

Human Autonomy Study is a separate research initiative. This Exit Framework page exists only to make the boundary explicit.

Visit humanautonomystudy.org →

Core boundary

No paid research access.

Buying Exit Framework, Founder 100 or Control Room does not create eligibility, informed consent or a guaranteed research place.

Commercial data ≠ research consent.

A checkbox requesting recruitment information is not study enrolment, research consent or a clinical assessment.

Expansion

Capacity follows integrity.

Any expansion of the research program should depend on protocol quality, operating capacity, data integrity, participant safety and required approvals — never on a commercial launch deadline.
