Field note 06 / Environment

Environment Shapes Outcome

Change the conditions that make the old behavior easy.

Environment is not background. It is an active decision system that presents cues, creates friction and makes some actions easier than others.

What is easy, visible and immediately rewarded will often defeat what is important but distant.

Map the behavior path

Choose one recurring behavior and trace what happens before it. What is visible? Which device is open? Who is present? What action requires the fewest steps?

Remove cues before resisting them

Move the object, remove the app, change the route, silence the notification or create a pause. The goal is not to prove that you can resist indefinitely. It is to stop paying for an avoidable contest.

Add friction to high-cost behavior

A delay, password, limit, block or required check-in can interrupt an automatic sequence long enough for choice to return.

Make the desired action ready

Prepare the training clothes. Leave the notebook open. Keep food and water visible. Show the next task and hide the rest. Good environments reduce the distance between intention and first action.

Design for the bad day

Test the environment under reduced capacity. Can the minimum viable action still be found? Are dangerous or distracting options still one tap away? A resilient environment becomes more protective as capacity falls.

This field note is educational. Environmental design should not replace professional support, safety planning or medical care.
