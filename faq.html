FAQ / Open protocol

Public core. Finite founding layer.

EXIT is deliberately simple at the boundary: the protocol is public, Founder 100 is limited, and research stays separate.

What is Exit Framework?

An open operating protocol for human autonomy under load. The core loop is: stabilize, observe, separate, choose, execute, measure, document and adjust.

What is public?

EXIT Language, the core protocol, Chaos Audit, Minimum Viable Day, Daily Mission, 30-Day Control Sprint and Control Room are public. You do not need Founder status to use the system.

Why make the core public?

EXIT becomes more useful when the language and operating model can spread without a transaction. Distribution, shared language and practical adoption matter more than manufacturing scarcity around information.

What is Founder 100?

A permanent founding cohort limited to 100 positions. Founder status is about identity, belonging, provenance and participation in EXIT's founding era — not access to the core method.

Will Founder 100 ever become Founder 250 or Founder 1000?

No. The Founder 100 designation ends at 100. The number is part of the meaning.

What does a Founder position include?

The final package will be defined before it opens. The current direction includes a serialized Founder number, verifiable founder record, a tangible Founder Edition, Founder Build Log access and a structured feedback channel. Nothing is sold before those elements are concrete.

Can I buy Founder 100 now?

No. There is no live Founder checkout yet. You can signal interest through the EXIT list and receive the launch notice when the package, price and delivery terms are ready.

Is EXIT therapy or medical treatment?

No. EXIT is an operating and self-management framework. It is not diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for qualified medical or mental-health care.

Does Founder status include Human Autonomy Study participation?

No. Research participation cannot be purchased, bundled or guaranteed. Recruitment, suitability and consent are governed separately.

Boundary

Research remains independent.

Product language, founder identity, lived experience and scientific evidence must remain distinguishable.

Research participation is not sold.

Human Autonomy Study has separate recruitment and consent.
