The difference

One day proves a move. Thirty days tests the system.

This is not a streak, course or motivation challenge. It is one persistent control loop run across enough real days to reveal what survives changing conditions.

Daily

State → constraint → bounded move.

Read the operating state, narrow the active load, make one bounded move and record what changed.

Weekly

Let evidence change the architecture.

Inspect drift, friction and low-capacity performance. Remove what fails repeatedly. Preserve what survives.

Day 30

Extract the rules that earned their place.

Turn thirty days of evidence into a personal operating layer: protected base, Not Now, re-entry protocol, decision rules, warning signals and next direction.

Designed for imperfect capacity

Graceful degradation is part of the system.

As capacity falls, scope contracts before force increases. Missed days remain data, not debt. Re-entry starts from the state that exists now.
