FAQ

Clear answers before checkout.

The launch is explicit about what exists now, what is planned and what remains separate.

What is Exit Framework?

A personal operating system for stabilizing the base, observing reality, separating facts from assumptions, choosing the highest-leverage move, executing it, measuring the result, documenting what happened and adjusting the system.

What is Founder 100?

The first 100 commercial Founder positions. Founder 100 includes Exit OS, 12 months of Control Room, a permanent founder number, build-log access and priority for future Field Layer beta invitations.

Is Founder 100 pricing live?

Not until checkout is activated. No payment is accepted here. Follow launch updates for confirmed pricing and availability.

Are the hoodie and field bag already in production?

No. Founder Drop 01 is reservation-first. Paid inventory should open only after samples, unit economics, sizing, fulfilment and returns are validated.

Are Oura or AR discounts included?

Not yet. They are planned partner benefits only and must not be presented as confirmed until written commercial terms exist.

What is Exit Field Layer?

A future ambient interface layer for bringing missions, status and context-aware prompts into wearable or AR environments. It is not required for the current launch.

Does Founder 100 include a place in Human Autonomy Study?

No. Research participation cannot be purchased, bundled or guaranteed. Commercial members may request recruitment notifications; actual participation is governed independently by protocol, eligibility, consent and capacity.

Is Exit Framework therapy or medical treatment?

No. Exit Framework is a self-management and operating-system product. It is not diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for medical or mental-health care.
