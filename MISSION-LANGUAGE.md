# The Exit Mission — language guide

## Identity

**The Exit Mission** is the only public master brand. Promise: **One mission. Your own way out.** People use free tools to protect stability, regain usable choice and keep what works; the mission never requires loyalty or membership.

- **Mission Plan**: public practical method page. Preserve the technical route `framework.html` for existing inbound links.
- **Mission Cycle**: eight moves — stabilize, observe, separate, choose, execute, measure, document, adjust.
- **Chaos Audit**: free six-question entry tool, not a diagnosis.
- **Control Room**: optional workspace, not a command hierarchy.
- **Daily Mission / 30-Day Control Sprint**: practical plans without missed-day debt.
- **Field Notes**: public reflections, clearly distinguished from scientific findings.
- **Founder 100**: optional numbered founding record. Support, not authority or personal rank.

## Language and safeguards

Speak to people as independent adults. Military/operations language describes planning, never control over other people. No artificial urgency, leader worship, secret levels, loyalty tests, clinical promises or unsupported science. Community contribution is opt-in. Criticism and departure require no permission. A successful user can walk away with the tools and no dependence on us.

Research participation is never sold. Human Autonomy Study remains organizationally and operationally separate, with its own recruitment, consent, privacy and governance. The Exit Mission does not diagnose or replace health care.

The subtle operations map is a decorative planning metaphor, not real location tracking. Do not alter data keys, users' browser state, account identity, functional routes or third-party URLs just to rename the brand.

## Acceptance checks

- No visible `Exit Framework` or `EXIT FRAMEWORK` in public HTML.
- Header, footer, SEO, social preview, navigation and CTAs use one brand.
- `framework.html` still works as Mission Plan; the eight steps are unchanged.
- Local-first tools, privacy boundary, founder limits and research separation remain intact.
- Legibility and navigation work on mobile, desktop and keyboard.
- No live-domain release until the appropriate production deployment and QA are verified.
