About

Build systems that preserve choice under load.

EXIT exists for the gap between intention and execution: the point where load, capacity, environment and feedback determine whether choice remains usable. It is structurally separate from Human Autonomy Study.

Mission

Build systems for human autonomy

Translate autonomy from an abstract value into operating conditions: fewer active variables, clearer constraints, stronger feedback and more usable choice.

Method

Turn experience into evidence

Capture decisions, outcomes, interruptions and adaptations so experience becomes evidence that can be reviewed instead of a story reconstructed later.

Standard

Integrity before scale

Keep lived experience, product language, hypotheses and research claims separate. Scale only what can survive scrutiny.

Destination

Long-term freedom

The destination is not control for its own sake. It is a wider, more durable set of choices across health, work, attention, relationships, income and place.

The architecture

Research asks. EXIT operates. Life Archive remembers.

High Priority AS provides the organizational and commercial structure. The boundaries matter: research, product and personal evidence should reinforce each other without becoming the same thing.

Human Autonomy StudyThe separate research mission and longitudinal study direction.
Exit FrameworkThe practical operating system for turning insight into action.
Life ArchiveThe evidence, documentation and learning layer.
High Priority ASThe commercial and organizational structure.

Research boundary

Useful first. Testable always.

Exit Framework is educational and experimental. It does not diagnose, treat or replace professional medical or psychological care. Personal observations are not universal findings, and claims should remain proportional to the available evidence.
