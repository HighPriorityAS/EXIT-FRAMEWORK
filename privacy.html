Privacy

Data should have a job.

This launch notice explains the intended data flow before final CRM and Stripe connections are activated. It should be reviewed against the production stack and applicable legal requirements before full payment launch.

What does the Founder form collect?

Name, email, country, selected commercial interest, optional note and a separate yes/no field for future HAS recruitment notifications.

What happens before a CRM endpoint is connected?

The launch form stores the current payload only in the browser session and redirects to the configured newsletter/launch-list fallback. The fallback provider then handles its own subscription flow.

What happens when Stripe is connected?

The website sends the visitor to a verified Stripe-hosted Payment Link. Payment-card data should be handled by Stripe rather than this static website.

Is HAS notification interest research consent?

No. It is only a request to receive recruitment information. Research consent and any study data collection must occur through a separate research process.

Are research data collected through the Founder checkout?

No. The commercial form must not be used to collect research measurements, clinical data or study consent.

What still needs final review?

The final CRM provider, retention rules, processor agreements, legal basis, contact details and production privacy wording should be reviewed before live payment and scaled data collection.
