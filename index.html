The problem

Most people do not have a motivation problem.

They have a system that leaks attention, energy, time and decision quality faster than motivation can replace them.

01 / Attention

Open loops

Unfinished tasks, forgotten promises and invisible obligations keep pulling working memory back into yesterday.

02 / Input

Dopamine chaos

Notifications, feeds and compulsive checking turn attention into a reactive resource instead of an intentional one.

03 / Capacity

Scattered energy

Sleep, recovery, stress and physical state determine which plans are actually executable today.

04 / Resources

Time and money leaks

Small unmanaged losses compound into fewer options, more friction and less autonomy.
