What Founder means

Do not pay to unlock the philosophy. Take a position in its origin.

The protocol, language, Sprint and Control Room stay open. Founder 100 is a separate layer of identity, provenance and participation. Its value should survive even if you never need another software feature.

01 / Identity

A permanent marker.

FOUNDER / 0XX OF 100 is not a subscription tier. It is a durable identity marker tied to EXIT's founding period.

02 / Belonging

A finite cohort.

One hundred people share the same founding moment, the same language and a closer line into how the system develops.

03 / Provenance

A place in the record.

Each position receives a serialized Founder number and a verifiable founder record. Public naming will be optional.

04 / Scarcity

100 means 100.

The Founder 100 designation does not become 250, 1,000 or a second “Founder” series. Scarcity is structural, not a countdown widget.

Founder rules / non-negotiable

Scarcity only matters when the rules survive success.

If EXIT becomes much larger, the founding cohort should become more historically meaningful — not larger. These rules exist to prevent dilution later.

Hard capExactly 100 Founder positions.
NumberOne serialized number per position. No duplicates.
CoreThe public protocol is not withheld to manufacture Founder value.
UrgencyNo fake timers, fake reservation counters or synthetic scarcity.
RecordFounder provenance remains verifiable after the cohort closes.
FutureNo second Founder 100 after the first hundred are assigned.

Founder layer

Make the identity tangible.

The final Founder package will be defined before any payment opens. The direction is deliberately physical and archival: things that prove the founding position rather than another bundle of gated content.

  • Public attribution only by choice
Edition

  • No invented partner perks
