Human Autonomy Study

Research stays separate from commerce.

HAS is a separate research initiative exploring autonomy, recovery, self-regulation, behavior and long-term freedom. Formal recruitment must follow an appropriate protocol, consent process, eligibility criteria and research capacity.

Core boundary

No paid research access.

Buying Exit Framework, Founder 100 or Control Room does not create eligibility, informed consent or a guaranteed place in HAS.

Commercial data ≠ research consent.

An email checkbox for recruitment notifications is only a request to receive information. It is not study enrolment, research consent or a clinical assessment.

Planned expansion pathway

10 → +25 → +65

Expansion is conditional on protocol quality, operating capacity, data integrity, participant safety and whatever approvals are required for the actual study design.

Phase 01

10

Validate the operating protocol and data-quality workflow.

Phase 02

+25

Expand only after the first phase is stable and reviewable.

Phase 03

+65

Scale toward 100 when the system can support it responsibly.

Rule

Integrity first

No commercial launch deadline should force research expansion.
