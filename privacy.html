Privacy

Data should have a job.

This launch notice describes the intended data flow. Production CRM, payment processing, retention rules and processor agreements must be reviewed against the final stack and applicable legal requirements before full live activation.

Does this website collect Founder details?

The Founder form is not open. This website does not collect names, email addresses, reservations or research interest. The launch-updates link takes you to Substack, where you choose separately whether to subscribe.

What happens before the CRM endpoint is connected?

No details are submitted or stored here. Subscribing on Substack is a separate action governed by that service’s terms and privacy information.

What happens when Stripe is connected?

The site sends the visitor to a verified Stripe-hosted Payment Link. Payment-card data should be handled by Stripe rather than this static website.

Is research-notification interest research consent?

No. It is only a request to receive recruitment information. Research consent and study data collection must happen through a separate research process.

Are research data collected through Founder checkout?

No. The commercial form must not be used to collect research measurements, clinical data or study consent.

What still requires final production review?

The final CRM provider, retention rules, processor agreements, legal basis, company contact details and production privacy wording.
