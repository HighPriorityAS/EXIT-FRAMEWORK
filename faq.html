FAQ / Open protocol

Your mission is yours. The tools stay open.

Use the Mission Plan alone or alongside others. Founder 100 is optional, and research stays separate.

What is The Exit Mission?

A free Mission Plan for people who want more choice in their own lives. The Mission Cycle has eight steps: stabilize, observe, separate, choose, execute, measure, document and adjust.

What is public?

EXIT Language, the core protocol, Chaos Audit, Minimum Viable Day, Daily Mission, 30-Day Control Sprint and Control Room are public. You do not need Founder status to use the system.

Why keep the Mission Plan free?

Autonomy should not depend on a paid tier, a leader, or a private community. Take the tools, improve them when useful, and leave whenever you like.

What is Founder 100?

Up to 100 numbered records of early support. They document provenance, not rank. Founders may contribute, but they do not control anyone else's mission.

Will Founder 100 ever become Founder 250 or Founder 1000?

No. The Founder 100 designation ends at 100. The number is part of the meaning.

What does a Founder position include?

Every position includes a unique Founder number from 001–100 and a verifiable place in EXIT's founding record. The physical Founder Edition, price and delivery terms will be published before Founder 100 opens.

Can I buy Founder 100 now?

Not yet. You can join the EXIT list to receive the launch notice when Founder 100 opens.

Is EXIT therapy or medical treatment?

No. The Exit Mission offers educational self-management tools, not diagnosis, treatment or a substitute for qualified medical or mental-health care.

Do I have to join or follow anyone?

No. Use the public tools anonymously. Community participation is voluntary, feedback and criticism are welcome, and leaving never requires permission. No hidden levels or loyalty tests.

Does Founder status include Human Autonomy Study participation?

No. Research participation cannot be purchased, bundled or guaranteed. Recruitment, suitability and consent are governed separately.
