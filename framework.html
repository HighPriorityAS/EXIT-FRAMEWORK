The framework

Control is built in stages.

Exit Framework is an operating model for moving from unstable reaction to disciplined autonomy. It replaces improvisation with a sequence that can be repeated, measured, and improved.

Stage 01 / Diagnose

See the pattern clearly

Chaos becomes manageable when it stops being vague. Separate facts from assumptions and identify the inputs creating instability.

  • Current-state audit.
  • Trigger and failure-loop mapping.
  • Identify the highest-leverage constraint.
Stage 02 / Stabilize

Protect the base layer

Recovery stability is the primary constraint. Sleep, safety, medication adherence, nutrition, and reduced volatility come first.

  • Minimum viable day.
  • Environmental safeguards.
  • Escalation rules for risk and overload.
Stage 03 / Structure

Install repeatable control

Structure converts intention into predictable behavior through missions, protocols, and visible feedback loops.

  • Daily mission and weekly review.
  • Decision filters aligned with autonomy.
  • Visible commitments and checklists.
Stage 04 / Prove

Build identity from evidence

Confidence becomes durable when it is supported by repeated proof, not temporary emotion.

  • Track completed missions.
  • Measure leading indicators.
  • Archive evidence and learning.
Operating loop

Observe. Decide. Execute. Archive.

Each pass produces better information, a clearer decision, measurable action, and documentation that improves the next pass.

ObserveMeasure reality without negotiation.
DecideSelect one highest-leverage next move.
ExecuteAct through a system that survives low motivation.
ArchiveTurn experience into data, evidence, and IP.
